NSF GRFP — reference model

submittedused as the template

The wording every other block is modelled on.

What NSF asked for

Verbatim
What to include in your letter
If you have been nominated to submit a reference letter for an applicant, NSF requests that you provide the following information in your letter:
The name of your department and institution.
How long you have known the applicant and in what capacity. If you are the candidate's research supervisor, please comment on the originality of their research statement, and what role, if any, you played in assisting the student with the application.
Comments on the applicant's potential for contributing to a globally engaged U.S. science and engineering workforce.
Statements about the applicant's academic potential and prior research experiences.
Statements about the applicant's proposed research.
Any other information to enable review panels to evaluate the application according to the NSF Merit Review Criteria (be sure to address both the Intellectual Merit and Broader Impacts criteria of the applicant).
Note that the more specific (as opposed to generic) a letter you can provide, the better reviewers can evaluate the candidate.

UC Berkeley — Biophysics

Mon 30 Nov · 8:59 pm PT3 minimum, up to 5

Route gradapp.berkeley.edu → Biophysics Graduate Group. Every element of the file, letters included, is due on the same date.

What program guidance exists

Letters "should be from persons who have supervised your research or academic work and who can evaluate your intellectual ability, creativity, leadership potential and promise for productive scholarship." Where a postdoc or graduate student supervised day to day, the faculty member in charge must also support the letter.

Request text

Block
Berkeley Biophysics requires three letters, with up to five accepted. Berkeley is the earliest deadline on this list: everything, letters included, is due Monday, November 30, 2026 at 8:59 pm Pacific (10:59 pm Central).

Berkeley's guidance is that letters come from people who supervised the applicant's research or academic work and can evaluate intellectual ability, creativity, leadership potential, and promise for productive scholarship.

Content most relevant to this application:
1. The supervision relationship and its duration.
2. Specific research contributions, and the creative decisions behind them.
3. Leadership: mentoring, collaboration, or initiative within a group.
4. Intellectual ability and promise for productive scholarship, measured against peers.
5. Where a postdoc or graduate student supervised day to day, the faculty member's oversight — Berkeley asks for that support to be explicit.

UCSF — Biophysics

Mon 1 Dec · 11:59 pm PT3 letters, confidential

Route gradapp.ucsf.edu → Molecular, Cellular & Computational Biophysics. Letters are submitted directly by the writers.

What program guidance exists

Letters "should ideally come from prior supervisors who can speak to their potential, independence, research experiences, and character." The program reads them in coordination with the rest of the file rather than scoring them separately, and notes that writers are best asked in advance whether a strong letter is possible.

Request text

Block
UCSF Biophysics requires three confidential letters, submitted through the application by the writers. The program's guidance is that letters ideally come from prior research supervisors who can speak to potential, independence, research experience, and character, and that letters are read alongside the transcript, statement, and research summary rather than scored on their own.

Content most relevant to this application:
1. The relationship — course, lab, summer project, or thesis — and its duration.
2. The specific project(s) and what Vincent actually did: techniques, decisions, troubleshooting.
3. Independence: where the work was driven, designed, or recovered from a setback.
4. Potential and trajectory, with comparison to peers at the same stage.
5. Character and working style, as they bear on a long collaborative PhD.

Deadline: Monday, December 1, 2026, 11:59 pm Pacific.

MIT — Biology

Tue 1 Dec · 5:00 pm ET3 letters, ≥2 academic

Route apply.mit.edu. One application per department; three letters, at least two academic.

What program guidance exists

Letters "should include details that highlight: Past research experience and/or scientific experience; Preparation for graduate school, with an emphasis on biology."

Request text

Block
MIT Biology requires three letters, at least two of them academic. MIT asks that each letter include details that highlight past research and/or scientific experience and preparation for graduate school, with an emphasis on biology.

Content most relevant to this application:
1. The relationship: course, lab, or project, and its duration.
2. The research question, the methods, and the part of the work that was Vincent's.
3. Research aptitude: how a failed experiment, a self-taught method, or a collaboration was handled.
4. Preparation for a biology PhD specifically: breadth, quantitative comfort, seminar participation, writing.
5. Assessment of potential relative to other students at a comparable stage.

Deadline: Tuesday, December 1, 2026, 5:00 pm Eastern (4:00 pm Central).

Stanford — Biosciences (Biophysics home program)

Tue 1 Dec · 11:59 pm PT3 required, up to 4

Route applygrad.stanford.edu → Biosciences, with Biophysics as the home program. Letters are submitted online by the writers.

What program guidance exists

Stanford asks applicants to "ask for a recommendation from at least one individual who can address your potential for original and creative research" — in practice the current or most recent research mentor. Three letters are required; a fourth is accepted.

Request text

Block
Stanford Biosciences, with Biophysics as the home program, requires three letters and accepts a fourth. Stanford specifically asks for at least one letter from someone who can address potential for original and creative research, which is why this request is going to a research mentor.

Content most relevant to this application:
1. The relationship and its duration.
2. The research question, and the part of it that was genuinely Vincent's.
3. Originality and creativity: where something was proposed, reframed, or found by an unexpected route.
4. Technical and intellectual preparation for a biophysics PhD.
5. Assessment of potential, with comparison to peers.

Deadline: Tuesday, December 1, 2026, 11:59 pm Pacific (1:59 am Central on December 2).

What program guidance exists

Under How we evaluate applications, the program lists letters as "External Evaluations (Recommendation Letters) – Evaluation of applicant performance, growth, and potential; concrete supporting examples; consistency of evaluation across referees."

Request text

Block
UChicago's Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering, applied to through PSD, requires three letters. Its deadline is Monday, November 30, 2026 at 11:59 pm Central, published in Central time.

PME describes letters as external evaluations of an applicant's performance, growth, and potential, and says it weighs concrete supporting examples and the consistency of evaluation across referees.

Content most relevant to this application:
1. The relationship, its capacity, and its duration.
2. Performance: the concrete work, and what was delivered.
3. Growth over the period of acquaintance.
4. Potential for research in an interdisciplinary, engineering-adjacent setting.
5. Candid comparison with peers, since the program reads letters against one another and calibration helps.
